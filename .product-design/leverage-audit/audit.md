# Leverage and density audit

## Verdict

The stable cross-DEX memory is not a particular color or border treatment. It is the order of decisions: margin mode and leverage are compact settings; the trader chooses order type and direction; enters one size; adjusts allocation; optionally adds risk controls; then commits. HyperEarn previously exposed both margin and size plus two unrelated sliders, which made the panel read as a configuration form.

## Captured steps

1. **Hyperliquid — healthy.** Compact Cross/10x/Unified settings precede Market/Limit, direction, one Size field, allocation, optional risk controls, and CTA.
2. **Variational — healthy.** 50x and Cross are compact top controls. One Size field and one allocation rail drive the order.
3. **Lighter — healthy.** Direction and order type lead into one Amount field, percentage allocation, optional TP/SL/Reduce Only, and CTA.
4. **VOOI — healthy.** 10x and Market are compact selectors above one Size field and allocation presets.
5. **HyperEarn before simplification — needs improvement.** Margin and Size were both editable, and leverage plus allocation used similar full-width sliders. This created duplicate decisions and visual competition.
6. **HyperEarn after simplification — healthy.** Margin mode and leverage are compact selectors, Size is the single exposure input, allocation remains familiar, optional TP/SL is pre-commit, and the CTA stays visible.

## Accessibility notes and limits

The new selectors are native controls with visible focus treatment, preserving keyboard and assistive-technology behavior. Screenshots establish hierarchy and visible contrast, but a full screen-reader and keyboard-order audit remains separate work.
